## 1. Add a short undo window before sending the anonymous alert

Adding a crush currently commits a slot and sends an alert right away. It is easy to select the wrong contact or mistype a number, and the form says the crush cannot be removed once added. After adding someone, show an Undo action for a short window and hold the alert until it expires. If the user undoes the action, release the slot and discard the pending alert. This gives people a chance to correct an honest mistake before it affects anyone else.

## 2. Offer separate preferences for incoming alerts and mutual matches

Settings groups alerts for anonymous additions and mutual matches under one switch. Split these into two independently configurable preferences. Someone who prefers to avoid ambiguous pings could mute anonymous additions and still hear about a mutual match. This gives people more control over interruptions while preserving Coy’s rule that a one-sided like never reveals the sender.
